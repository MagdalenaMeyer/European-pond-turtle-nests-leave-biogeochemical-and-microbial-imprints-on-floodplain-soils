# Nests of the endangered European pond turtle leave a biogeochemical and microbial imprint on floodplain soils

Here we deposit the data and R code presented in the upcoming research paper entitled "Nests of the endangered European pond turtle leave a biogeochemical and microbial imprint on floodplain soils".

We provide three datasets (Dataset_Nutrients_EmysOrbicularis_Meyer, Dataset_16S_EmysOrbicularis_Meyer, and Dataset_ITS_EmysOrbicularis_Meyer), which form the basis for the analysis of soil nutrient, biogeochemical, bacterial, and fungal community data associated with nests of the endangered European pond turtle (Emys orbicularis).

We also provide five R code files used for the analyses: nutrients, 16S, nutrients_meet_16S, ITS, and nutrients_meet_ITS.

Dataset_Nutrients_EmysOrbicularis_Meyer contains 43 columns containing information on each soil sample as rows:

A. sample_ID (a unique identifier for each soil sample) B. sample_type (type of soil sample collected) C. season (season during which the sample was collected) D. nesting_site (nesting site where the sample was collected) E. nest_depth (depth of the nest) F. total_egg_number (total number of eggs recorded in the nest) G. egg_number_hatched (number of eggs that successfully hatched) H. egg_number_undeveloped (number of undeveloped eggs) I. failure_unknowncause (number of eggs or hatchlings that failed for unknown reasons) J. dead_embryos/hatchlings_nest (number of dead embryos or hatchlings recorded in the nest) K. living_hatchlings_nest (number of living hatchlings recorded in the nest) L. hatching_rate (proportion of eggs that successfully hatched) M. emergence_rate (proportion of hatchlings that successfully emerged from the nest) N. P1 (phosphorus in the first fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) O. P2 (phosphorus in the second fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) P. K1 (potassium in the first fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) Q. K2 (potassium in the second fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) R. CA1 (calcium in the first fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) S. CA2 (calcium in the second fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) T. MG1 (magnesium in the first fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) U. MG2 (magnesium in the second fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) V. BOR1 (boron in the first fraction, mg/1000 g, elemental form) W. BOR2 (boron in the second fraction, mg/1000 g, elemental form) X. NA (sodium in the first and second fractions combined, mg/100 g, elemental form) Y. TON (clay content) Z. HUMUS (humus content, calculated as total organic carbon (%) × 1.724) AA. S_ICP1 (sulphur measured by ICP in the first fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) AB. S_ICP2 (sulphur measured by ICP in the second fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) AC. P_ICP1 (phosphorus measured by ICP in the first fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) AD. P_ICP2 (phosphorus measured by ICP in the second fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) AE. AML1 (amount of extract in ml at the anode, first fraction) AF. AML2 (sum of extracts at the anode and cathode in ml, first fraction) AG. FML1 (amount of extract in ml at the anode, second fraction) AH. FML2 (sum of extracts at the anode and cathode in ml, second fraction) AI. NO3_DE (nitrate-N in the first fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) AJ. Norg_DE (organic N, calculated as total N minus nitrate, first and second fractions combined, mg/100 g, elemental form) AK. Norg1_DE (organic N, calculated as total N minus nitrate, first fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) AL. Norg2_DE (organic N, calculated as total N minus nitrate, second fraction, mg/100 g, elemental form) AM. MIKROML (amount of extract in ml, third fraction) AN. FE3 (iron in the third fraction, mg/1000 g, elemental form) AO. MN3 (manganese in the third fraction, mg/1000 g, elemental form) AP. ZN3 (zinc in the third fraction, mg/1000 g, elemental form) AQ. CU3 (copper in the third fraction, mg/1000 g, elemental form)

Dataset_16S_EmysOrbicularis_Meyer contains 18 columns containing information on bacterial sequencing samples and associated nest parameters as rows:

A. feature_ID (unique identifier of the bacterial sequence feature) B. tube_ID (unique identifier of the sample tube) C. sequencing_ID (unique identifier used during sequencing) D. nest_ID (unique identifier of the nest) E. sample_type (type of sample collected) F. nesting_site (nesting site where the sample was collected) G. sampling_date (date on which the sample was collected) H. sampling_year (year in which the sample was collected) I. season (season during which the sample was collected) J. nest_depth (depth of the nest) K. total_egg_number (total number of eggs recorded in the nest) L. egg_number_hatched (number of eggs that successfully hatched) M. egg_number_undeveloped (number of undeveloped eggs) N. failure_unknowncause (number of eggs or hatchlings that failed for unknown reasons) O. dead_embryos/hatchlings_nest (number of dead embryos or hatchlings recorded in the nest) P. living_hatchlings_nest (number of living hatchlings recorded in the nest) Q. hatching_rate (proportion of eggs that successfully hatched) R. emergence_rate (proportion of hatchlings that successfully emerged from the nest)

Dataset_ITS_EmysOrbicularis_Meyer contains 17 columns containing information on fungal sequencing samples and associated nest parameters as rows:

A. feature_ID (unique identifier of the fungal sequence feature) B. tube_ID (unique identifier of the sample tube) C. nest_ID (unique identifier of the nest) D. sample_type (type of sample collected) E. nesting_site (nesting site where the sample was collected) F. sampling_date (date on which the sample was collected) G. sampling_year (year in which the sample was collected) H. season (season during which the sample was collected) I. nest_depth (depth of the nest) J. total_egg_number (total number of eggs recorded in the nest) K. egg_number_hatched (number of eggs that successfully hatched) L. egg_number_undeveloped (number of undeveloped eggs) M. failure_unknowncause (number of eggs or hatchlings that failed for unknown reasons) N. dead_embryos/hatchlings_nest (number of dead embryos or hatchlings recorded in the nest) O. living_hatchlings_nest (number of living hatchlings recorded in the nest) P. hatching_rate (proportion of eggs that successfully hatched) Q. emergence_rate (proportion of hatchlings that successfully emerged from the nest)

The R code files contain the scripts used to perform the statistical analyses and generate the outputs presented in the associated manuscript:

1. nutrients (analysis of soil nutrient and biogeochemical data)
2. 16S (analysis of bacterial community data)
3. nutrients_meet_16S (combined analysis of soil nutrient and bacterial community data)
4. ITS (analysis of fungal community data)
5. nutrients_meet_ITS (combined analysis of soil nutrient and fungal community data)
